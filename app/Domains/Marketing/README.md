# Marketing Domain: M0/M1 foundation

Marketing owns campaign drafts and paid advertising observations. Growth retains OpportunityCandidate and governed Outreach; Product owns catalog/stock/price; Sales owns lead/deal facts; Finance owns monetary commitments and accounting.

Current scope:
- Tenant-disabled module manifest, canonical capability contracts, MySQL draft/revision/idempotency/outbox persistence.
- Meta GET-only adapter and Vault-backed connection/sync service. No account may be controlled by COS on import.
- No paid mutation capabilities, Meta activation routes, Finance Expense writes, or independent executor.
- Manual integration acceptance, Finance F1 and canonical Workflow gates are pending.

Read docs/04-domains/marketing/overview.md for implementation/acceptance levels.
