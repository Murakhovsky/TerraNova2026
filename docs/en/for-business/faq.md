# COS FAQ

## Does COS replace CRM?

Not necessarily. CRM can remain the main tool for contacts and sales while COS coordinates the wider process around it.

## Do we need to move all data into COS?

No. Each data source is evaluated separately. Some data may be moved, some may only be read, and some should remain in the original system.

## Is AI required?

No. COS can manage processes, rules, roles and automation without AI.

AI is added where interpretation, summarization, classification or assisted decision-making creates real value.

## Can AI make decisions by itself?

Only within authority defined for a specific action.

Critical actions can require human approval or be completely blocked from automatic execution.

## Can we start with one process?

Yes. That is usually the recommended approach.

One well-chosen process is enough to test the operating model, integrations and business value before expanding.

## Do we need to replace our existing software?

No. Existing systems can remain in place when they have a reliable way to connect.

## What companies benefit most?

COS is most useful when a company has several teams, repeated processes, multiple systems, a lot of manual coordination or growing dependence on individual employees remembering what to do next.

## Is COS a finished universal product?

COS is an actively developed platform. Some areas are already implemented and others are being expanded.

The practical rule is simple: evaluate the specific process and capability you plan to use rather than assuming every possible business domain is already complete.
